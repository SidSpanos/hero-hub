The record

Nothing here is guessed at

Every number comes off a screenshot of the game and lands in one file that the whole app reads from. When a figure is missing, the card says so rather than filling the hole.

01

Shoot the tooltip

Each hero has five slots — four skills and an awakened ultimate. Every tooltip carries a DMG Factor, an upgrade preview across five levels, and the conditions it fires under. Ninety-three screenshots started this; new heroes arrive the same way.

02

Transcribe into the sheet

One block per hero in core-skills.js — the authority. It holds both halves: a hand-kept table of troop, talents, skin and warband, and every skill's effect lines with all five upgrade values. Two files used to claim to know a hero's talents; they disagreed on 31 rows of 35 before anyone noticed. Now there is one.

03

The app reads, and places

Add a hero to that file and they appear on all nine views, score themselves, and drop into the tier their score earns. Drop portraits/Ganglot.png in a folder and they have a face. No code is edited to add a hero.

The rule

Every skill scores at least 3

The good ones score 4, the ones that decide a fight score 5 — then skin and meta on top. That is the whole model. It reads the skills and disagrees with the tier board where it disagrees, which is the point of having it.

WORTH = five skills (1–5 each) + skin (5) + meta (0–5) + your call (±)

Everyone is sorted by it and the tiers fill in the sizes the board already uses, so the best score always lands in the best tier. A tie goes to the better kit — the five skills alone, before skin or meta — because those are things about a hero and the skills are the hero.

5
How the hero kills. A payload of 8,000+, a summon, or defence-down.
4
A real multiplier or real sustain. Damage-up 15%+, counterattack 10%+, healing, mitigation 10%+.
3
It does something, but it is small. A 3 has not been missed — it has been weighed.
+1
Each skill inside a trifecta. Three of a side is a trifecta, four a quadfecta — 18 heroes hold one, 4 hold the larger.

A finding

Hit hard, or strip armour. Almost nobody does both

Payload against defence ignored, every hero plotted. The two multiply — a big hit into stripped armour lands for far more than the same hit into a full set — so the top-right corner is where a hero would want to be. It is empty except for one.

Kept honest

What it does not know

A card that pretends is worse than a card that admits. These are on the face of the app, not buried in a comment.

Unread slots

A hero whose tooltip was never opened scores nothing for it and the card says unread. Baldur sits on 8 because four of his five have never been shot — not because his kit is poor.

Blank is not zero

Power is recorded only at level 60 and 6 stars, so it is one yardstick. A hero short of that stays blank, and the card counts how many of the 37 are actually in.

A ceiling, not a scale

Ignoring 50% of a target's defence and ignoring 7% both score 5, because 5 is the top. The COMPANY card prints the real magnitudes beside the score, where the scale cannot reach.

Not everything is read

67 of the 181 skills carry effects no marker looks at — rage, movement, proc chances — and fall to 3 by design. The model reads a short list of things that decide fights.
