How the Focus ranking is worked out

In the Roster, sort by Focus to see where your levels, stars, skills and gear pay back most. Only heroes you own are ranked. Tier dominates on purpose — it's the hero's own strength you're buying — and everything else refines the order rather than overturning it.

A tier step is worth more than any amount of team membership, so a B-tier can't outrank an S just by appearing in your pairings. Two things override that: what you actually meet — the Meta tallies, the biggest single term — and whether you already own someone better. You field five teams, so roughly two heroes per troop earn their keep; the third is a reserve and the fourth is a bench warmer, however good they'd be on their own. Pick up a stronger hero of that troop and everyone below slides down a slot the moment you tick the box.

Hero Score

One card per hero, one score out of 5 for everything worth scoring — five skills, three talents, any troop, and how often they turn up in the meta. Rate them as a commander and again as a lieutenant, since the two seats aren't the same hero. The TALLY card on the right of each row adds it all up in front of you, and R 7/10 in the header says how much of that score is yours rather than a default. Pick a mode first — a talent that wins sieges does nothing in arena.
